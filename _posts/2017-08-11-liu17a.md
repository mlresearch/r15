abstract: 'In this paper we study an optimal stopping policy for a multi-agent delegated
  sequential matching system with fairness constraints. We consider a setting where
  a mediator/decision maker matches a sequence of arriving assign- ments to multiple
  groups of agents, with agents being grouped according to certain sensitive attributes
  that needs to be protected. The deci- sion maker aims to maximize total rewards
  that can be collected from above matching process (from all groups), while making
  the matching fair among groups. We discuss two types of fairness constraints: (i)
  each group has a cer- tain expected deadline before which the match needs to happen;
  (ii) each group would like to have a guaranteed share of average reward from the
  matching. We present the exact char- acterization of fair optimal strategies. Example
  is provided to demonstrate the computation ef- ficiency of our solution.'
