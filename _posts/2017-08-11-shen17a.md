abstract: Subset selection tasks, such as top-k ranking, induce datasets where examples
  have cardinal- ities that are known a priori. In this paper, we propose a tractable
  probabilistic model for sub- set selection and show how it can be learned from data.
  Our proposed model is interpretable and subsumes a previously introduced model based
  on logistic regression. We show how the parameters of our model can be estimated
  in closed form given complete data, and propose an algorithm for learning its structure
  in an in- terpretable space. We highlight the intuitive structures that we learn
  via case studies. We finally show how our proposed model can be viewed as an instance
  of the recently proposed Probabilistic Sentential Decision Diagram.
