abstract: "$\\ell$1-Graph, which learns a sparse graph over the data by sparse representation,
  has been demonstrated to be effective in clustering especially for high dimensional
  data. Although it achieves compelling performance, the sparse graph generated by
  $\\ell$1-Graph ignores the geometric information of the data by sparse representation
  for each datum separately. To obtain a sparse graph that is aligned to the underlying manifold structure of the data, we propose the novel Neighborhood Regularized
  $\\ell$1-Graph (NR$\\ell$1-Graph). NR$\\ell$1-Graph learns sparse graph with locally
  consistent neighborhood by encouraging nearby data to have similar neighbors in
  the constructed sparse graph. We present the optimization algorithm of NR$\\ell$1-Graph
  with theoretical guarantee on the convergence and the gap between the sub-optimal
  solution and the globally optimal solution in each step of the coordinate descent,
  which is essential for the overall optimization of NR$\\ell$1-Graph. Its provable
  accelerated version, NR$\ell1$-Graph by Random Projection (NR$\ell1$-Graph-RP)
  that employs randomized data matrix decomposition, is also presented to improve
  the efficiency of the optimization of NR$\ell1$-Graph. Experimental results
  on various real data sets demonstrate the effectiveness of both NR$\\ell$1-Graph
  and NR$\ell1$- Graph-RP. This work is supported in part by US Army Research Office grant W911NF-15-1-0317. The work of Jiashi Feng was supported by NUS startup
  R-263-000-C08-133, MOE R-263- 000-C21-112 and IDS R-263-000-C67-646. Pushmeet Kohli
  was at Microsoft Research during this project."
