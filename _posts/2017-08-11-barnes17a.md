abstract: In this paper, we study the non-IID learning setting where samples exhibit
  dependency within latent clusters. Our goal is to estimate a learner’s loss on
  new clusters, an extension of the out-of-bag error. Previously developed cross-validation
  estimators are well suited to the case where the clustering of observed data is
  known a priori. However, as is often the case in real world problems, we are only
  given a noisy approximation of this clustering, likely the result of some clustering
  algorithm. This subtle yet potentially significant issue afflicts domains ranging
  from image classification to medical diagnostics, where naive cross-validation is
  an optimistically biased estimator. We present a novel bootstrap technique and
  corresponding cross-validation method that, somewhat counterintuitively, injects
  additional dependency to asymptotically recover the loss in the independent setting.
